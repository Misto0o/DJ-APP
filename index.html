🎵 Loading Music

  • Use “+ Add” in the Crate to upload MP3/WAV files
  • Click A or B to load a track

🎚️ Deck Controls

  • ▶ Play – Start / pause the track
  • ⏮ Cue – Jump back to cue point
  • Jog Wheel – Scratch / nudge the track
  • Pitch Slider – Adjust BPM manually

🔥 Hot Cues

  • Set hot by pressing LEFT SHIFT + 1-8
  • Deck A uses hot cues 1–4
  • Deck B uses hot cues 5–8
  • Instantly jump to saved cue points

🎛️ Mixer

  • Crossfader – Blend between Deck A and B
  • Volume sliders – Control deck loudness
  • Center = both decks equal
  • Left = only Deck A audible
  • Right = only Deck B audible

⚡ Quick Transitions

  • Sync BPM – Match both deck tempos
  • Smooth – Automatic fade transition
  • Cut to A/B – Instant switch
  • Fade A/B – Fade out a deck smoothly

📥 MP3 Downloader

  • Search for songs or paste a YouTube URL
  • Download audio and add it directly to your crate
  • YouTube & YouTube Music supported

🛞Jog Wheels

Click and drag the jog wheels to scratch or nudge tracks. Press play again to resume normal playback.
